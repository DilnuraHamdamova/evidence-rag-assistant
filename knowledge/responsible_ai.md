# Responsible AI

## Human oversight

AI output is decision support, not an automatic final decision, for employment, education, credit, health, housing, or legal use cases. A qualified person must review high-impact recommendations and users must have a clear escalation path.

## Data and privacy

Collect only data needed for the documented purpose. Do not place secrets, private customer records, or regulated personal data in prompts unless the approved architecture and agreement explicitly cover that data.

## Transparency

The interface must identify AI-generated content, describe important limitations, and show evidence when the task depends on retrieved documents. Unsupported answers should be refused rather than presented as fact.
